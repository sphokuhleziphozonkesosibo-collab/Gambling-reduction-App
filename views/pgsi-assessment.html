🩺 Gambling Screening (PGSI)

This is the Problem Gambling Severity Index — the same standardized 9-question screening tool used by health professionals. It takes about 2 minutes and helps BetAware SA tailor your experience. Answer honestly — there are no wrong answers, and nothing here is shared beyond your own account.

Question 1 of 9
Thinking about the last 12 months, have you bet more than you could really afford to lose?
Question 2 of 9
Still thinking about the last 12 months, have you needed to gamble with larger amounts of money to get the same feeling of excitement?
Question 3 of 9
When you gambled, did you go back another day to try to win back the money you lost?
Question 4 of 9
Have you borrowed money or sold anything to get money to gamble?
Question 5 of 9
Have you felt that you might have a problem with gambling?
Question 6 of 9
Has gambling caused you any health problems, including stress or anxiety?
Question 7 of 9
Have people criticized your betting or told you that you had a gambling problem, whether or not you thought it was true?
Question 8 of 9
Has your gambling caused any financial problems for you or your household?
Question 9 of 9
Have you felt guilty about the way you gamble, or what happens when you gamble?
Please answer all 9 questions before submitting.
