🇿🇦 National Self-Exclusion Guide

Under South African Law (National Gambling Act), you have the legal right to ban yourself from all licensed casinos, bookmakers, and betting sites simultaneously for 6 months or permanently.

How to complete Self-Exclusion:
1. Call SARGF Toll-Free: 0800 006 008
2. Request the National Self-Exclusion Form.
3. Once submitted, all SA bookmakers (Betway, Hollywoodbets, etc.) will legally close your accounts.
In-app exclusion: Confirming below will immediately disable all bet-logging buttons in BetAware SA for 6 months. This does NOT remove the button by mistake — it can only be undone by contacting SARGF directly, on purpose.
